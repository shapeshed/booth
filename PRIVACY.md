# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-13

Booth is a local-first podcast player. This policy explains what the app stores and which
network requests it makes when you use its features.

## Summary

Booth does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, Firebase, or mandatory user accounts. The app
does not sell personal data.

## Data stored on your device

Booth stores podcast subscriptions, episode metadata, playback positions, downloads, and settings
in the app's private storage. This data is not sent to the
developer. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Network requests

When you add or refresh a podcast, Booth connects directly to the podcast's RSS or Atom feed URL.
When you stream or download an episode, Booth connects directly to the media URL published by the
podcast. It may also request show artwork and episode images.

These requests are made to third-party feed, publisher, image, and favicon hosts. Those operators
may receive technical information such as your IP address, request time, device network details,
and the URL requested. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

Booth does not operate a proxy between you and those hosts.

## Notifications

If enabled, Booth uses Android media notifications for playback and may report new episodes from
refreshed subscriptions. On Android
versions that require it, the app requests notification permission. Notification content is derived
from the feeds you have chosen to follow.

## OPML

Booth can import and export subscriptions when you choose those actions. Exported OPML files are
created at the location you select. You are responsible for how you store or share them.

## Children

Booth is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Booth's features or data handling change. For privacy questions,
open an issue in the private repository or contact the repository owner.
